About me

I started in a bank’s contact centre and spent seven years there, coaching new hires and handling escalations from high-net-worth clients. I then led a resolution team that dealt with card disputes, failed transactions and fraud cases, before moving into corporate relationship management.

Across all three roles, the work was the same: spot trouble early, own the problem, and leave the client trusting us more than before. That is customer success, whatever the job title said.

I’m based in Lagos, work comfortably with distributed teams, and can overlap with CET and US time zones. I’m open to Customer Success, Client Success and Onboarding roles with global teams.

Course case study: onboarding, feedback and journey

Sunrise Digital MFB: one joined-up customer experience review

A fast-growing microfinance bank had a new app, four scattered feedback channels and four weeks to give its Board a single view of the customer experience.

What I found

  • Verification was the biggest loss by headcount, and customers who lost a session had no way back.
  • Failed transfers, repeated logouts and no human contact were eroding trust after sign-up.
  • Feedback sat in four channels with no single owner.

What I did

  • Mapped one customer’s journey from awareness to loyalty, showing where trust is earned or lost.
  • Designed a voice-of-the-customer process: capture, classify, theme, prioritise, assign and close the loop.
  • Built a four-phase roadmap with an owner for each phase.

What I recommended

  • Fix transaction reliability and status communication first.
  • Redesign onboarding around save-and-resume, clarity and confirmation.
  • Put one owner in charge of customer feedback.
Course case study: merchant retention and recovery

Akwete Pay: winning back 312 inactive merchants

A payments company serving 2,400 Onitsha market merchants saw cluster volume fall 21% in three months while its failure rate climbed from 2% to 7%.

Tools used

What I found

  • Of 40 merchants interviewed, 18 cited late settlement and 11 cited slow reversals. Only 7 cited competitor offers.
  • Some reversal tickets had been open more than five days.
  • The data pointed to service problems, so leading with discounts would have been the wrong move.

What I did

  • Wrote a six-step recovery playbook: validate, prioritise, contact, diagnose, resolve, reactivate.
  • Set up a 90-day plan with daily, twice-weekly and weekly reviews, and a KPI set.
  • Built a HubSpot view tracking each merchant’s recovery status and root cause.

The priority account

  • For Ifeoma Superstores: acknowledge, investigate, assign an owner, communicate, confirm and retain.
  • Target of 80% of baseline volume, checked at day 7 and day 30.
  • The 25% reactivation goal is a planning target, not a forecast.
Course case study: proactive customer success at scale

Okonkwo FinTech: an early-warning system for BukkiBooks

One attentive Customer Success executive saved an account by noticing five weeks of silence. The question was how to do that for thousands of SME accounts.

What I researched

  • How Moniepoint, Paystack and Bumpa use activity and transaction data to show where customers need attention.
  • Takeaway: a drop in usage is a reason to investigate, not proof of churn.

What I built

  • An automation flow from product usage to CRM, health score, segment, task and Slack alert for the team.
  • A health score dashboard and a prioritisation view that ranks accounts by risk.
  • A clear split between Customer Success (proactive) and Support (reactive), with a shared feedback loop.

The risks I planned for

  • Seasonality and business changes can make low usage misleading.
  • A human reviews every alert before any action, so false positives do not become wrong calls.
